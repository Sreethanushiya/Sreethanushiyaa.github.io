Clubs & Forums

A club is an association of people united by a common interest or goal. A forum is an online
discussion board where people can ask questions, share their experiences, and discuss
topics of mutual interest. Forums are an excellent way to create social connections and a
sense of community and belonging, pursue individual interests, and gain tangible
experience and skills. Forum aims at going beyond classroom learning and promoting a
creative bent of mind, ingenious thinking and a genuine appreciation of the challenges of
the wider world.
The college has a number of forums, clubs and such other assemblies which help students
and teachers to sharpen their abilities and to build capacities in various aspects life skills.
Over all development of the personality is the prime focus in all these endeavours. Joining
an on-campus club can provide personal, social, and professional benefits.
Though there are a number of forums and clubs functioning on the campus. In most cases,
you should join 1-2 on-campus clubs and make real commitments.
